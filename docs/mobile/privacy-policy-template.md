## Overview

Cambio (“we”, “our”, “the app”) is a multiplayer card game. This policy describes what information the app and website collect and how we use it.

## Information we collect

### Information you provide

- **Display name** — shown to other players in your game room
- **Room codes** — you create or enter codes to join games

We do not require an account, email, or password to play.

### Information collected automatically

- **Gameplay and connection data** — room membership, game state, and chat messages are processed on our servers to run multiplayer games
- **Technical data** — IP address, device/browser type, and timestamps may be logged by our hosting providers for security and reliability
- **Analytics (if enabled)** — [Vercel Analytics / Speed Insights — describe if enabled on cambio.brierton.ie]

We do **not** sell personal information. We do **not** use third-party advertising trackers in the native app shell.

### Local storage on your device

- Player identifiers and preferences (theme, sound settings) may be stored in browser or WebView storage to support reconnect and settings

## How we use information

- Operate multiplayer games and match players in rooms
- Maintain game state and enforce rules
- Improve reliability and fix bugs
- Respond to support requests

## Where data is processed

Game servers run on **Cloudflare Workers** (global edge network). The web app may be hosted on **Vercel**. Data may be processed in regions where these providers operate.

## Data retention

- Active game rooms and state are ephemeral and removed when rooms close or idle out
- Server logs are retained for a limited period for operations and security [specify retention, e.g. 30 days]

## Children

Cambio is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at [support@brierton.ie](mailto:support@brierton.ie).

## Your choices

- You can stop using the app at any time
- Clear app data or uninstall to remove locally stored preferences
- Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence
